# Small Business Website Rescue

What I found and fixed on the websites of two family-owned businesses: a Brooklyn kitchen showroom and an airsoft arena company.

**Kitchen showroom**
- Every online order was also being sent outside the company. Shut that off and finished the broken order flow.
- 2,828 bot orders cancelled, 73 old callbacks closed, 24 out-of-stock products hidden.
- 4 new contact and quote forms. Every request reaches the inbox within seconds.
- Customers can buy from the Instagram and Facebook shop.
- An AI assistant on the site, and faster pages.

**Airsoft arenas**
- Every online order was also emailed to a former contractor. Closed it.
- Cyber Monday tickets: last year 41 orders were coded and emailed by hand. Now each ticket gets its code automatically when payment goes through.
- Regular audits, with every change reported to the owner in plain English.
